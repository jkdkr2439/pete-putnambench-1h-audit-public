# Scope and claim boundary

This is an observational pilot, not a controlled comparison against another model and not evidence of mathematical superhuman performance.

The public claim is limited to what the retained artifacts establish:

1. A 673-item corpus was inventoried before timing.
2. A one-hour answer-free scheduling and proof-locking run was recorded.
3. 178 natural-language candidates were frozen before reference access.
4. Subsequent audit produced 100 result agreements, 72 manual self-audit passes, 3 gaps, 2 failures, and 1 reference conflict.
5. Three pre-run contaminated items and 492 deferred items receive no credit.
6. No proof entered Lean, Coq, Isabelle, or another proof kernel; formal verification is 0/673.

No causal claim about the private framework follows from this single run. No claim about another model's performance follows without a matched experiment.
